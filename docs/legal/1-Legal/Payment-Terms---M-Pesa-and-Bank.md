## Summary
Payment instructions, confirmation, reconciliation, and responsibility for M-Pesa and bank transactions.

## How payments work
M-Pesa STK initiation may be available where configured. The provider callback confirms settlement; bank/cash entries require owner reconciliation.

## Ownership of funds
Unless a separate operating agreement says otherwise, rent payments are made to the owner's own account, not PropertyFlow [TO BE CONFIRMED].

## Confirmed payments
A receipt is issued only after confirmation or owner reconciliation.

## Failed or duplicate payments
Unconfirmed funds are not represented as paid. Duplicate or mismatched entries require review and may remain in a reconciliation queue.

## Receipts
Receipts are system records and do not replace bank or Safaricom statements.

## PropertyFlow role
PropertyFlow is not a bank, M-Pesa agent, payment institution, or settlement guarantee.
