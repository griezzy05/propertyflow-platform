## Summary
The owner uses PropertyFlow to manage tenancy records, rent, maintenance, and communications. This statement explains what tenants can see and do.

## What is managed
The owner or its authorised staff may view your unit, rent and balance, lease dates, requests, receipts, communications, and related documents.

## What tenants can do
Tenants may activate their account, change their password, view their own home and lease, submit maintenance/safety/recommendation requests, view request status and payments, and upload permitted repair attachments.

## Visibility
Tenant accounts do not receive another tenant's private data. Owners and assigned agents see only information needed for their roles.
