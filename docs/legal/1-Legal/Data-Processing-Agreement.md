## Summary
This DPA defines PropertyFlow's processing of tenant personal data on behalf of a property owner acting as controller.

## Roles
Owner is controller. PropertyFlow is processor for the data hosted and processed at the owner's instruction.

## Instructions
PropertyFlow processes tenant data only to provide property management, support, security, and legally required functions.

## Confidentiality
Personnel and service providers with access must be authorised and bound by confidentiality.

## Subprocessors
PropertyFlow may use Render/database hosting, Resend, Africa's Talking, Safaricom Daraja, and private S3-compatible file storage. Changes and locations are recorded in the Vendor Register.

## Security
PropertyFlow uses account authentication, role-based access, password hashing, private storage, short-lived links, audit history, encrypted transport, and backups.

## Breach notification
PropertyFlow will notify the owner without undue delay, and in any event provide the available incident information promptly enough to support assessment and ODPC notification within 72 hours [VERIFY WITH ADVOCATE].

## Data subject requests
PropertyFlow will assist the owner with access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability requests.

## Deletion and return
At instruction or contract end, data is returned or deleted unless law requires retention.

## Audit
The parties may review relevant controls and records. Regulated audits require reasonable notice and information-security safeguards.
