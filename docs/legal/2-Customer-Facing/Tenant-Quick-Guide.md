## Payments
Choose an enabled payment method configured by your owner: M-Pesa to their phone or Paybill/Till, M-Pesa to their bank account, bank transfer, or cash. Pay the owner directly using the displayed details, then submit the transaction code or cash receipt number. The payment remains pending until your owner verifies it; only confirmed payments count as paid. Do not approve an STK prompt unless the recipient displayed by Safaricom matches the intended owner.
