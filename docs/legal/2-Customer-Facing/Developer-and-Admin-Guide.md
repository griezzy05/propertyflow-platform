## Owner service payment ledger
Use the Developer dashboard's **Platform service payments** section to record payments received from an owner for PropertyFlow service. Enter the owner, amount, payment date, payment method, and receipt or transaction reference. New entries remain pending until the developer verifies the payment outside the app and confirms it; only confirmed entries count toward total received revenue. Rejected and pending entries remain visible in the ledger but are excluded from the received total.

This manual ledger does not initiate or verify M-Pesa payments. It is separate from tenant rent records and does not redirect tenant rent to PropertyFlow. Record only amounts actually received for the platform service.
